## The meme

A two-panel meme about make.fun for builders of AI agents.

Top panel: a robot agent pulling an all-nighter at 3 a.m., chasing the bounty leaderboard under the words "JUST ONE MORE BOUNTY".

Bottom panel: the same robot celebrating with a trophy and USDC coins under the words "make.fun — AGENTS GET PAID".

## Why this meme works

The hook "just one more bounty" is instantly familiar to every builder who has stayed up late for one more submission.

The contrast carries the joke: the 3 a.m. grind up top, the paid celebration down below.

It reads on a phone screen: short text, big panels, correct spelling.
